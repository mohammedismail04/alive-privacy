Alive Privacy Policy

Health, wellness, avatars and community — with transparency about your data.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Alive mobile application ("Alive", "we", "our") handles personal information. For questions, contact mdismail.arfath@gmail.com.

Before publishing: This draft must be checked against Alive's actual app behavior, service configurations and Google Play Data safety answers. Remove any data categories or services that the app does not use, and add any that are missing. In particular, confirm Health Connect, avatar/selfie processing, public profile visibility, retention and account deletion functionality.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, Alive may process:

  • Account and profile details: information associated with sign-in, such as your account identifier, name, email address, profile details and profile photo.
  • Wellness information: health and lifestyle information you enter, such as sleep, stress, digestion, energy, goals and progress.
  • Physical activity: step counts or related activity information, if you enable compatible device permissions or Health Connect access. Access is subject to the permissions you grant.
  • Avatar information: your selected or generated avatar, avatar assets and images, and any photos you choose to provide for avatar creation.
  • Community content: posts, reactions, challenges, participation and other content you choose to share.
  • Technical information: information needed for app functionality, security, troubleshooting and service operation, which may include device identifiers, app version and diagnostic information.

2. How we use information

We use relevant information to provide sign-in and account features, display health and activity information, generate and display avatars, operate social and challenge features, save your preferences and progress, maintain security, troubleshoot errors and respond to support requests. We do not use health data for advertising unless we separately disclose a lawful and permitted use and obtain any required consent.

3. Permissions and Health Connect

If enabled in your app version, Alive requests access to step data through Android Health Connect using the android.permission.health.READ_STEPS permission. If enabled, Android activity recognition may also be used for supported physical activity features. You can manage or revoke permissions in Android settings or Health Connect. Alive should request only permissions needed for features it actually provides.

4. Sharing and visibility

Information you deliberately publish through social features, such as posts or shared avatar content, may be visible to other users according to the app's visibility settings. Avoid posting sensitive information publicly. We may use third-party technology providers to process data on our behalf for hosting, authentication, avatar generation, media delivery and other app functions. We do not sell your personal health information.

5. Service providers

Alive uses, or may use depending on the released build, services such as Google Firebase for authentication and app data storage, avatar-generation services such as Avaturn, and media hosting services such as ImageKit or Cloudflare. Each provider may process information necessary to deliver its function. The final list of providers and any international transfers must be confirmed before publishing.

6. Data storage, security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. Exact retention periods and deletion behavior should be confirmed against the production implementation.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may update information using available in-app features, manage device permissions and request access to or deletion of your account and associated personal information by emailing mdismail.arfath@gmail.com with the subject "Alive Account Deletion Request". We may need to verify account ownership before processing a request. Certain information may be retained where legally required. Before publication, ensure this process works and add a publicly accessible account-deletion webpage and in-app deletion route where Google Play policy requires them.

8. Children's privacy

Alive is not intended for children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete it. Any age eligibility and parental-consent rules in the released app must match this statement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, providers or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted at this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact: mdismail.arfath@gmail.com.
