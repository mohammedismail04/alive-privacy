Alive Privacy Policy

Health, wellness, avatars and community — with transparency about your data.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alive ("Alive", "we", "us"), a health and lifestyle mobile application, collects, uses, processes, discloses and deletes information when you use our services. For privacy questions or requests, email mdismail.arfath@gmail.com.

1. Information we process

  • Account and profile information: When you use Continue with Google, we receive account information made available through sign-in, which may include your name, email address, profile image and a unique account identifier. We associate your Alive profile with a user ID.
  • Health and wellness entries: Information you choose to record, such as sleep, stress, digestion, energy, habits, goals, daily progress, streaks and related history.
  • Steps and activity data: If you enable compatible activity features and grant the required permissions, Alive may access step counts and related physical activity information through Android activity recognition or Health Connect, as applicable to the installed version.
  • 3D avatar data: Your account ID and the URL of your generated avatar, which we store to retrieve and display your avatar. Avatar-related display images or content you deliberately share may also be processed as part of those features.
  • Social and challenge information: Profile details, posts, shared avatar content, reactions, challenges, goals, participation and reward activity that you create or interact with.
  • Store, coupon and order information: Items selected, coupons issued or redeemed, and information necessary to process orders and provide delivery or customer support, such as delivery and contact details where requested during checkout.
  • Technical and operational information: Information required for authentication, app operation, security, troubleshooting and service performance, which may include device identifiers, app diagnostics and service logs depending on the SDKs active in your app version.

2. Selfies and avatar creation

To create a personalized 3D avatar, you choose to upload a selfie through an integrated third-party avatar creation service, such as Avaturn. Alive does not store or retain your original selfie photograph in its own Firebase database or storage. The selfie is submitted to and processed by the avatar creation provider to generate the avatar. That provider may handle or retain the selfie according to its own terms and privacy practices, which are separate from ours.

Alive stores your user ID and generated avatar URL in Firebase so that the avatar can be displayed when you use the app. The generated avatar may be hosted or delivered through third-party infrastructure. If you choose to publish an avatar image or animation in a social post, that content may be visible to the audience you select or to other users according to the app's sharing settings.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate users, maintain accounts and display personalized profiles and avatars.
  • Provide health dashboards, step tracking where enabled, history, goals, streaks and lifestyle insights.
  • Operate social posts, interactions, challenges, leaderboards and community features.
  • Calculate and display earned rewards and coupons, and apply eligible coupons to purchases in the Alive store.
  • Manage physical-product orders, fulfillment, delivery and support where those features are offered.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot technical issues and improve app reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy and deletion requests.

4. Rewards, coupons and store purchases

Alive may award coupons or other in-app rewards for qualifying activities or challenges. We process the relevant participation, progress and redemption records to determine eligibility and prevent duplicate or fraudulent redemption. Coupons may be used toward eligible food or other physical products in the Alive store, subject to the offer's displayed terms. For purchases, the information needed to complete the order may be shared with payment, merchant, fulfillment or delivery providers involved in that transaction. The payment provider's own privacy terms may also apply. We do not claim that rewards are cash or transferable unless the offer expressly says so.

5. When information is shared

We use third-party service providers where necessary to operate Alive. These may include Google Sign-In and Firebase for authentication and app data; Avaturn or the avatar creation provider for processing selfies and generating avatars; and avatar hosting or media delivery services such as ImageKit where configured. Physical-product checkout, payment processing and delivery may involve additional providers when those services are enabled.

Information is provided to those parties as needed for the relevant feature. Information you intentionally publish in social features, such as posts or shared avatars, may be visible to other users. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect the safety and integrity of our service. We do not sell users' personal health information or original selfies.

6. Health and activity information

Health and activity information is used to provide user-requested health and wellness features. If Alive requests access to step counts through Health Connect or Android activity recognition, the relevant access depends on your device permissions and enabled features. You can manage permissions through your device settings and, where applicable, Health Connect. We do not use Health Connect information for advertising or sell it to data brokers. Alive is a wellness service and is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or treatment.

7. Data security and retention

We use platform and service-provider safeguards, including access controls and secure network connections where supported, to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain account and feature data while it is needed to provide Alive, unless you delete your account or a longer retention period is legally required. Some limited records may need to be retained where necessary to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or complete transactions. Third-party providers may have their own retention schedules, including for selfies processed during avatar creation.

8. Your choices and account deletion

You may choose whether to enter wellness information, create an avatar, participate in social activities or grant optional activity permissions. You may also request access to, correction of or deletion of your information by contacting us.

Alive provides a Delete Account option inside the app. According to Alive's account-deletion process, deleting your account removes your Firebase Authentication account and associated Alive account data, including stored health history, social posts and avatar URL. Certain information may remain only where legally required, and third-party providers may independently retain data under their own policies. You may also request deletion from outside the app by emailing mdismail.arfath@gmail.com with the subject "Alive account deletion request". We may need to verify that the request concerns your account before acting.

For instructions on requesting account deletion, see our Account Deletion page.

9. Children and audience

Alive is intended for users who are permitted to use the app under the age requirements stated in its app listing and applicable law. If you believe a child has supplied information contrary to those requirements, contact us so we can review and address the issue.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our features, service providers or legal obligations change. We will publish the revised version at this address with an updated date. If a change materially affects your privacy, we will provide additional notice where required.
