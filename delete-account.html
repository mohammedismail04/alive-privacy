Delete Your Alive Account

Request deletion of your Alive account and associated data

Option 1: Delete inside Alive

Sign in to Alive, open the app's account or profile settings, select Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

Email mdismail.arfath@gmail.com with the subject Alive account deletion request. Include the email address associated with your Alive account so we can locate it. Do not send passwords, identity documents or verification codes. We may request reasonable verification before deleting the account.

What is deleted?

Deleting your Alive account removes your Firebase Authentication account and associated Alive data, including health history, social posts and your stored avatar URL. Data that must be retained to satisfy legal obligations, prevent fraud or complete transactions may be retained only as necessary. Third-party avatar providers may have separate retention practices for selfies processed by them.

For more information, read the Alive Privacy Policy.
